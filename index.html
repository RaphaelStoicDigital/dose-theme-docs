Daily Dose theme documentation

Daily Dose is a Shopify theme for supplement, wellness and botanical brands. It helps shoppers understand what is inside each product and choose with confidence: supplement facts panels, ingredient spotlights, a product quiz, a bundle builder and subscription-ready product pages.

Getting started

Install the theme

  1. In Shopify admin, go to Online Store → Themes.
  2. Add Daily Dose from the Shopify Theme Store and choose a style (Daily Dose or Botanical).
  3. Click Customize to open the theme editor. Preview your store before you publish.

Recommended first steps

  1. Upload your logo in Header and choose your colors in Theme settings → Colors.
  2. Create your main menu in Online Store → Navigation. Nested items appear as dropdowns.
  3. Create collections for the goals your products support, for example Sleep, Energy, Immunity and Focus. These work well in the Shop by goal section.
  4. Set up the supplement facts metafields so every product page shows its label.
  5. Optionally create a quiz page and a bundle page.
Most text, images and links on the demo store are set in the theme editor. Your own products, collections, pages and blog posts are added in Shopify admin and then connected to sections.

Header and footer

Header

  • Logo and Logo width. Without a logo, your store name is shown.
  • Menu: menu items with child links open as dropdowns on desktop and as expandable lists on mobile.
  • Customer account menu: shown in the account panel when customers are signed in. Daily Dose uses Shopify's customer accounts.
  • Country/region and language selectors appear when you sell in more than one market or language.
  • Sticky header keeps the header visible while scrolling.

Announcement bar

A short message above the header, with an optional link. Use it for shipping thresholds or current offers.

Footer

  • Menu blocks show a heading and a menu. Text blocks show a heading and short text.
  • Optional newsletter signup, Follow on Shop button, country/region and language selectors, payment icons and the "Powered by Shopify" link.

Pickup availability

When local pickup is enabled in Settings → Shipping and delivery, the buy buttons show whether the selected variant can be picked up and where.

Supplement facts setup

The facts panel reads from three product metafields, so each product shows its own label.

  1. Go to Settings → Custom data → Products and add these definitions:
    NameNamespace and keyType
    Serving sizecustom.serving_sizeSingle line text
    Servings per containercustom.servings_per_containerSingle line text
    Supplement factscustom.supplement_factsMulti-line text
  2. Open each product and fill in the metafields at the bottom of the page.
  3. In the theme editor, open a product page, select the Supplement facts block and connect each setting to its metafield using the dynamic source icon. The demo templates are already connected.

Nutrient row format

Write one nutrient per line, with columns separated by a vertical bar: name, amount and % Daily Value.

Magnesium (as magnesium glycinate) | 200 mg | 48%
Calm Blend | 350 mg |
- L-Theanine | 200 mg |
- Ashwagandha root extract | 150 mg |
Other ingredients
  • An empty % column shows a dagger (†), meaning a Daily Value is not established.
  • A line that starts with a hyphen is indented as a sub-ingredient.
  • A line without a vertical bar becomes a full-width group heading.

Choose the Label style for a classic bold-ruled panel or Clean for a lighter look. You can also show the panel inside a collapsible tab and add a footnote.

Subscriptions

Daily Dose shows subscription options when your products have selling plans. Selling plans are created by a subscription app, such as the free Shopify Subscriptions app.

  • In the Buy buttons block, turn on Show purchase options. Shoppers can choose one-time purchase or subscribe, with the savings shown.
  • Use Preselect subscription to select the subscription option by default.
  • The Subscription comparison section shows both prices side by side. If the product has no subscription plan, the section is hidden from shoppers and a notice is shown only in the theme editor.

Customers manage their subscriptions from their account.

Collections and search

  • Product grid (collection pages): products per page, columns, collection image and description, second image on hover, vendor, filtering and sorting.
  • Filters are set up with the free Shopify Search & Discovery app (for example by price, availability, product type or tag).
  • Predictive search suggests products, collections, pages and articles as shoppers type.
  • Collections grid lists all collections on /collections.

Product quiz

The quiz asks a few questions and recommends the product that matches the most answers.

  1. Go to Online Store → Pages, create a page (for example "Find your formula") and choose the quiz template.
  2. In the theme editor, open that page. In the Product quiz section, edit the intro and result text.
  3. Each Question block has up to four answers. Pick a recommended product for each answer.
  4. Add the page to your menu or link to it from the hero buttons.
When answers point to different products, the product chosen most often wins. If there is a tie, the product picked first wins.

Bundle builder

Shoppers pick several products and add them to the cart in one click, with progress toward discount tiers.

  1. Create a page (for example "Build your bundle") and choose the bundle template.
  2. In the Bundle builder section, choose the collection to show and set up to two tiers, for example 3 items for 10% off and 5 items for 15% off.
  3. Create matching automatic discounts in Discounts → Create discount → Amount off products: method Automatic, minimum quantity of items equal to the tier, applied to the same products.
The section only shows the tiers. The discount itself is applied at checkout by the automatic discounts you create. If you change the tiers, update your discounts too. Gift cards are never added to bundles.

Cart

  • Choose a cart drawer (opens over the page) or a cart page in Theme settings → Cart.
  • Shoppers can change quantities, remove items and apply discount codes. Codes that don't apply are removed with a short message.
  • Turn on Show order note on the cart page to collect special instructions.
  • Automatic discounts and subscription details are shown for each line.

Blog and pages

  • Blog posts: columns, topic filter (based on article tags), featured image, date, author and excerpt.
  • Blog post: featured image, author and estimated reading time.
  • Contact page: create a page with the contact template.
  • Gift cards: a dedicated gift card page shows the code and balance, with buttons to print it or add it to Apple Wallet.
  • Password page: logo, heading and optional newsletter signup while your store is closed.

Apps and custom content

  • The product page and the Custom section accept app blocks. Add them from the theme editor after installing an app.
  • Most text settings support dynamic sources, so you can connect them to metafields and metaobjects.
Code changes: editing theme code is not covered by support. If you customize the code, duplicate the theme first and keep a backup. Customized themes may not update cleanly to new versions.

Languages

Daily Dose includes storefront and theme editor text in English and Portuguese (Brazil). To change any storefront wording, go to Online Store → Themes → ⋯ → Edit default theme content. To sell in more languages, use Shopify's language settings and a translation app.

Performance and accessibility

  • Upload images at least 2000 px wide. Shopify serves smaller sizes automatically.
  • Add alt text to product and section images so screen reader users know what they show.
  • Keep the hero image as the first image on the page. It loads with high priority for a fast first view.
  • Install only the apps you need; each app can add scripts that slow the store down.
  • Interactive elements are keyboard accessible, and the theme respects the "reduce motion" setting.

FAQ

Why doesn't the supplement facts panel show on my product?

The panel is hidden when there are no nutrient rows. Check that the product has the custom.supplement_facts metafield filled in and that the block is connected to it. See Supplement facts setup.

Why don't I see subscription options?

Subscriptions need a subscription app that adds selling plans to your products. Also check that Show purchase options is on in the Buy buttons block.

The bundle builder shows a discount, but checkout doesn't apply it. Why?

The tiers in the section are only displayed to shoppers. Create matching automatic discounts in Shopify admin. See Bundle builder.

How do I add filters to collection pages?

Install the free Shopify Search & Discovery app, choose your filters there, and keep Enable filtering on in the Product grid section.

How do I change the quiz recommendations?

Open the quiz page in the theme editor, select each Question block and choose a recommended product for each answer.

How do I create a dropdown menu?

In Online Store → Navigation, drag a menu item under another item to nest it. The parent item becomes a dropdown.

Can I switch between the Daily Dose and Botanical styles?

Yes. Your products and content stay the same; only colors, fonts and shapes change.

How do I update the theme?

When a new version is available, Shopify shows it in Online Store → Themes. Updating adds a new copy of the theme with your settings, so you can preview it before you publish.

Support

Need help with Daily Dose? Send us a message through our support contact form. You'll get a copy of your message by email right away, and we reply within two business days, Monday to Friday.

Please include your store URL (for example your-store.myshopify.com), the page where you see the issue and a screenshot if possible.

Support covers theme setup, settings and bugs. It doesn't cover custom code changes, third-party apps or store design services.
